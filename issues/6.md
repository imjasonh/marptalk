To upload your video to YouTube with captions and chapter markers, follow these steps:

1. **Export the Video File**: 
   - Make sure your video is in a compatible format (such as MP4). Use your video editing software to export the final version of your video.

2. **Create an .srt File for Captions**: 
   - Write out the captions for your video in a text editor. Use the following format for the .srt file:
     ```
     1
     00:00:01,000 --> 00:00:03,000
     Caption text here.

     2
     00:00:04,000 --> 00:00:06,000
     Next caption text here.
     ```
   - Save the file with a .srt extension (e.g., captions.srt).

3. **Upload the Video to YouTube**: 
   - Go to YouTube and click on the upload button.
   - Select your exported video file to begin the upload process.

4. **Add Captions**: 
   - Once your video is uploaded, go to the "Subtitles" section in the YouTube Studio.
   - Click on "Add Language" and select your language.
   - Click on "Upload file" and choose your .srt file to add captions.

5. **Add Chapter Markers**: 
   - In the video description, add timestamps followed by titles to create chapter markers. Format it like this:
     ```
     00:00 - Introduction
     01:30 - Main Topic
     02:45 - Conclusion
     ```
   - Save your changes, and your video will now have both captions and chapter markers!
